The Problem

Students at IUGB are eating
in the dark.

Every day, students eat cafeteria meals without knowing if something on their plate could trigger a serious allergic reaction — and when something goes wrong, medical staff have no access to their history.

⚠️

Hidden Food Allergies

Cafeteria meals contain no ingredient labels. Students with allergies have no way to know what they're eating — until it's too late.

🏥

Medical Staff Flying Blind

Campus health services lack access to student medical history, leading to wrong or delayed treatment during emergencies.

⏱️

Every Second Counts

During an allergic reaction, minutes matter. Without fast data access, medical responses are dangerously slow.

The Solution

A smart health companion
built for Ivorian students.

Allexiria is a mobile app that stores each student's complete medical profile, connects it to their student ID, and sends real-time alerts when cafeteria food contains a known allergen.

01

Secure Medical Profile

Store allergies, blood type, ongoing treatments, and emergency contacts — all encrypted and linked to your student ID.

02

Daily Menu Scanning

Each day's cafeteria menu is cross-checked automatically against student allergy profiles before meals are served.

03

Real-Time Allergy Alerts

If a dish contains a dangerous ingredient for you, you receive a push notification before you enter the cafeteria.

04

Medical Staff Access

Campus health workers get instant access to a student's medical history during emergencies — with prior consent.

05

Emergency Contacts

In critical situations, the app automatically notifies designated emergency contacts and the campus health center.

06

Verified Health Info

Access curated medical information about treatments — no more relying on rumors or campus misinformation.

How it works

From enrollment to protection
in 4 steps.

1

Create your profile

Sign up with your IUGB student ID. Enter your allergies, blood type, and health conditions securely.

2

Cafeteria uploads the menu

Each morning, cafeteria staff upload the daily menu with full ingredient lists via the admin dashboard.

3

Allexiria scans for your allergens

The app cross-references your allergy profile against every ingredient in today's menu — automatically.

4

You eat safely

Receive a clear green/amber/red safety report before lunch, so you always know what's safe to eat.

Social Impact

Saving lives at IUGB
and beyond.

Allexiria is more than a convenience tool — it's life-safety infrastructure for African campuses that lack centralized health systems. Our goal is to eliminate preventable food-related emergencies and create a replicable model for universities across Côte d'Ivoire and West Africa.


This project was born from real incidents experienced by students at IUGB. Designed by students, for students.
